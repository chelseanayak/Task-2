# 📊 Superstore Profitability & Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 🎯 Project Objective

To transform raw Superstore sales data into a compelling visual report that identifies key business drivers, highlights areas of financial risk, and provides **actionable insights** for improving overall profitability.

## 💾 Data Source

* **File:** `Superstore.xlsx - Sample - Superstore.csv`
* **Time Period:** 4 Years (2014-2017)

## 📈 Key Visualizations

The report's narrative is built on the following core visualizations:

1.  **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, and **Overall Profitability Ratio**.
2.  **Sales Trend Line Chart:** Tracking growth, seasonality, and long-term momentum.
3.  **Sorted Column Chart (Profit vs. Category):** Identifying profit leaders (Technology) and loss leaders (Furniture).
4.  **Scatter Plot (Discount vs. Profit):** Analyzing profit leakage by correlating high discounts with negative profit transactions.
5.  **Filled Map (Geographic Profitability):** Highlighting specific states with negative profit margins (e.g., Texas, Illinois).

## 🔑 Key Business Insights

The analysis reveals that the **Overall Profitability Ratio ($\approx 12.5\%$)** is suppressed by localized operational issues:

* **Profit Leakage:** The **Furniture** category is a net drain on profit, primarily due to aggressive, unmanaged discounting practices.
* **Geographic Risk:** Financial losses are heavily concentrated in specific high-volume states (e.g., **Texas and Illinois**).
* **Recommendation:** Implement an immediate review of Furniture pricing policies and focus targeted strategic intervention exclusively on the loss-making geographic regions.
